This is the start of a new project called PageFlow

Problem Solving:

    Many people who sell services run into the same issue at a certain point
    they have the skillset (The skill being the service they sell) and clientele
    but they want to create a professional website.

    BUT 

    Here in lies the problem. You will need to either pay for a Website designer
    who is profecient with UI/UX design or create your own within something like
    Shopify or Wix. You will need to speed an ungodly amount of time creating your
    site which to take weeks or even MONTHS and sometimes hiring someone may start
    to get pretty expensive. DON'T even get me started on hard coding your own site.
    you then have to code everything from scratch and worry about where to host your
    site then do bug fixing etc

    This project will get rid of this entire step by provide ready to go websites
    that are already deployed and comes with a domain and payment functionality 
    All you will need to do is sign-up, pick a website template and input some info
    and the site will be generated and deployed for you.
